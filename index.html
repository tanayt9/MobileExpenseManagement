# Privacy Policy for Mobile Expense Management **Last updated: August 16, 2026** Mobile Expense Management ("the App") is an offline expense management application. ## Information We Collect The App does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with us or with third parties. The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information. ## Data Stored on Your Device Expense information and app settings may be stored locally on your device so that the App can provide its functionality. Any updated version will be published at this URL. ## Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing for the App.
